Building Your Weekly Practice Menu

A productive weekly rhythm for a MIKE is: one block-destruction progression daily, one movement / change-of-direction circuit, and one integrated situational period that layers coverage or blitzing onto a read. Tackling circuits belong in team warm-ups, and the Program Pursuit Drill is the highest-leverage five minutes a defense can spend.

Daily Non-Negotiables
🏋️
Block Destruction (daily)
Rotate: Shock/Lock/Shed Board → Button Press & Pop-Off → Get Off Block on Sled. Never skip — block destruction is the foundation every other skill is built on.
👟
Movement Circuit (daily)
Knowles Bag Progression or Glazier Six-Drill Circuit on odd days; Pleester Footwork Basics (5-Progression) or Combo Bag Drill on even days. Keep hips square, eliminate the crossover.
🎯
Program Pursuit (every practice)
5 minutes. Scout offense on air. Ball hits 25-yard cone ℔ all 11 sprint into hat-around in 4–6 seconds or repeat. The highest-leverage five minutes a defense can spend.
👁️
Eyes Before Feet
MIKE's greatest accelerator is the speed of his eyes. Half-Line Read and Play Reaction must come after block destruction so reads feed run fits directly, not the other way.
